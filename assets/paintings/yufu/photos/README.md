# 渔父图 — 展厅照片 / gallery photographs

Photographs of **渔父图 Fishermen’s Evening Song** taken in the gallery. Drop them in here; nothing
needs renaming first — `photos.json` beside them is what carries the meaning,
and it is written once a photo has been placed on the painting.

- Any filename. The originals off a phone (`IMG_4312.jpeg`) are fine.
- Keep the EXIF for now: the timestamp orders a visit, and the focal length
  helps guess magnification. It gets stripped when a photo is published.
- One photo can cover another; that is expected, and how to show two views of
  the same rock is the open question in `docs/detail-photos.md`.

`photos.json` is not written by hand — see that document.
